1 · Overview — What HOB actually is

Start here • 3 min

Magic: The Gathering | The Hobbit™ (HOB) — medium, 5-archetype Standard-legal Universes Beyond set. 193 draft cards (321 numbered with variants). Companion code HOC is eternal-only. First Tolkien set legal in Standard (unlike LTR). Preview debut Jul 18, full gallery Jul 31, GenCon pre-prerelease Jul 30–Aug 2, store prerelease Aug 7–13, Arena/MTGO Aug 11, tabletop Aug 14, Gift Bundle Sep 4.

At a glance
  • Boosters: 14-card Play Boosters (Draft = 3, Sealed = 6)
  • Format: 40 cards, 23 spells / 17 lands, BO3 with sideboard
  • Draft mode: Designed for Pick-Two (4 players); Arena also Pick-One Premier/Traditional
  • Speed: Assertive / curve-sensitive — need a 2-drop, but boards stall ~turn 4–5 (little flying, big ground)
  • Fixing: Typal duals + Hobbit Hole (Evolving Wilds) + Treasure — splash one single-pip, not double-pip

2 · Mechanics — Real rules, limited translation

Verified vs. release notes
⌕

Headline mechanics from The Hobbit Mechanics + Release Notes. “What it means” = pick / play advice.

📖 Storied — new keyword ability

Reminder: “If you control three or more artifacts, legendaries, and/or Sagas, you have an enduring story for rest of game.”

  • Need a permanent with storied while threshold met. Counts artifacts + legendary permanents + Sagas (multi-qualifier counts once).
  • Once gained, enduring story sticks forever — not a counter/emblem, losing board doesn’t remove it.
  • Payoff lives on other cards — storied itself does nothing alone.

Limited: Concentrated in RW Dwarves — legendary Dwarves + Equipment + Treasure + Sagas make 3 easy. Count cards not labels: legendary artifact = 1. Tokens that are Treasure/Axe count. Don’t force storied outside RW.

🪖 Recruit — new keyword action

To recruit: Draw a card, discard a card, if discarded card was nonland create a 1/1 white Human Soldier token.

  • Land discard = filter only, no token. Draw triggers “second card drawn” payoffs.
  • Flashback / graveyard cards soften cost. Multiple recruits resolve independently. Unlike connive, no +1/+1 counter.

Limited: Backbone of WU Humans (draw-two + go-wide). Recruit nonlands when body matters or discard is useful (Flashback, Adventure). Don’t recruit away your only removal to make a 1/1.

🔨 Hone counters — new counter type

Each hone counter on Equipment gives equipped creature +1/+0.

  • Stays on Equipment when unattached; does nothing while unattached. Moving Equipment transfers benefit (counters on Equipment, not creature).

Limited: RW Equipment deck snowballs — every hone stick makes later creatures bigger. Auto-attach Equipment wants you attacking, not durdling.

📜 Adventure — returning

Cast either half from hand. If Adventure resolves, exile card; later cast permanent from exile. Countered Adventure → graveyard, not exile. While on stack as Adventure, card elsewhere has only permanent’s characteristics.

Limited: Card economy king — Bofur // Concerted Care (hexproof+indestructible trick → lifelink body), Smaug the Great Calamity // Spew Flame etc. Adventure = effectively 2-for-1; prioritize over vanilla 3-drops.

👺 Amass Goblins N — returning (Goblin)

If no Army, make 0/0 black Goblin Army token, then put N +1/+1 counters on Army you control. Existing Army just grows; with multiples you choose.

Limited: Core of BR Goblins with sacrifice/death payoffs (e.g., Bolg of the North). Army = one big threat that dodges edict-style removal but hates bounce/exile. Don’t over-sac the Army itself.

🌿 Landfall — returning theme

Triggers when land enters under your control. Central to GU Elves (e.g., Silvan Reveler). Ramp/rebuy lands to trigger repeatedly.

Limited: GU is slowest archetype — needs actual landfall/Elf engines, not just “good green-blue cards”.

🐺 Power 4+ (ferocious-style)

Not a keyword here — BG Wolves / Mirkwood animals reward controlling power ≥4. Think rate + fight tricks.

Limited: Play efficient bodies; Quarrel-style punch spells are premium because deathtouch + power-matters synergize.

✨ Sagas / Treasure / Flashback / Cycling / Equipment / Ward

Sagas get lore counter after draw step, trigger chapter, sac after last. Treasure {T}, sac: add one mana of any color. Flashback, landcycling/typal cycling, Equipment, ward all recur — esp. RW.

Limited: Pinecone Strike / Burn, Burn, Tree and Fern can pop Equipment/Axe/Treasure for extra value. White’s removal is more conditional than black’s.

Sources: Wizards Mechanics & Release Notes; Vision Design; MythicSpoiler. Ring tempts you & historic do NOT return.

3 · Archetypes — The 5 Real Companies

Not 10 — HOB is 5 pairsPick-Two draft

HOB supports five allied/enemy pairs (gold signpost + hybrid uncommon/common + typal dual each). Off-archetype UB/UR control is speculation; start with these.

Filter:
W U — WU Humans: Recruit / Draw-Two / Tokens

Signposts: Bard the Bowman (gold) · Eagle’s Rescue (hybrid uncommon) · Patient Instructor (hybrid common) · Land: Lake-town (tapped W/U, sac 4 mana → two +1/+1 on Human)

Plan: Recruit repeatedly, trigger second-card-draw payoffs, convert Soldier tokens via counters/Equipment/team pumps.

Caveat: Potentially powerful but structurally risky — white common removal shallow, blue creatures uneven. Needs repeatable recruit, not just one trick.

B R — BR Goblins: Amass & Sacrifice

Signposts: Bolg of the North · Fearsome Goblin Pair · Goblin Plate Mail · Land: Goblin-town

Plan: Build one big Goblin Army, sac expendable creatures/artifacts, grind with death triggers + best-in-set black removal.

Take: Best early consensus = safest draft lane + deep removal. Versatile — can aggro or grind.

B G — BG Wolves: Power 4+ Matters

Signposts: The Chief Warg · Large Bear · Duskwatch Hunter · Land: Mirkwood

Plan: Play independently good bodies, enable ferocious-style bonuses, fight through board.

Strength: Straightforward + black removal; good card-quality deck when synergies miss.

R W — RW Dwarves: Storied & Equipment

Signposts: Thorin Oakenshield · Bifur, Melodic Rider · Nori, Teller of Tales · Land: Iron Hills

Plan: Curve legendary Dwarves + artifact makers + auto-attach Equipment → hit enduring story fast, attack.

Speed: Fastest deck — deepest two-drop roster (1-drop into 2-drop matters). Don’t durdle with late Equipment engines; density + early storied wins.

G U — GU Elves: Landfall

Signposts: Silvan Reveler · Thranduil, Sindarin Liege · Mirkwood Nurturer · Land: Elvenking’s Halls

Plan: Ramp/rebuy lands, repeated landfall growth, Elf engines take over long game.

Risk: Slowest & most demanding — needs real landfall/Elf engines, generic GU may fail. Often ranked lowest early.

Fixing: All five typal duals ETB tapped, tap for either color, sac for 4 mana to put two counters on matching type. Hobbit Hole = Evolving Wilds. Supports single-pip splashes, not 5-color. Off-archetype UB/UR value is hypothesis from Lords of Limited, not solved meta.
Pivot advice: Pick-Two means you draft 2 cards per pack — signals move faster. After 2–3 picks, count playables; if your pair is contested, neighboring pair with shared color is cheap pivot thanks to treasures/duals.

P1P1: Best card that keeps you in one color — avoid double-pip gold unless bomb. Early consensus: black > red > green > white/blue at common (provisional — before 17Lands matures).

Curve math (Wizards + experts)

  • Suggested curve: 1 mana 1–2, 2 mana 7–8, 3 mana 5–6, 4 mana 3–4, 5 mana 2–3, 6+ 0–1, 17 lands
  • Need 2-drop on turn 2 — RW/BR punish stumbles. Keep total creatures 13–16.
  • Pick-Two: you see more cards, commit slightly faster than normal draft.

Pack 2 — Find open lane

  • By P1P8: what’s wheeling? If zero good cards in your colors, pivot to neighbor sharing a color.
  • Take fixing (typal duals, treasure makers) once you have 8+ playables in pair.
  • Hate only bombs that beat you — not filler.
Stay if

8+ playables across pair, signals agree, you’re passing gold of other pairs freely.

Pivot if

<5 playables by pick 6 in a color, or late premium removal still wheeling elsewhere.

Pack 3 — Fill holes & cut

  • Need 2-drops? Take commons over medium rares.
  • 17 lands default; 16 if curve <2.8 + treasures; 18 if splash or 6+ five-drops.
  • Sideboard: duress, artifact hate, flying hate — important in BO3.

Signals in Pick-Two

Signals = what you’re passed. Late premium = open. Pick-Two doubles info per pack — late cards are stronger signal.

5 · Sealed — Build from Chaos

6×14 • 40 cards

Sort in 5 minutes

  1. Lay 84 cards by color (WUBRG + multi + artifact/land). Bombs face-up.
  2. Count playables ≥C per color. Ignore filler.
  3. Count fixing: typal duals, Hobbit Hole, Treasure makers.
  4. Find two deepest colors + best single-pip splash.
Rule: need ~14 playables per pair to make 23. Best pair 17+ = clean 2-color. Else plan splash. Black is tiebreaker when pools close (Slice + value creatures).

Build the 40

  • Bombs > synergy: Pool won’t give perfect synergies — lean bombs + removal + best commons.
  • Curve still king: Wizards 1/7-8/5-6/3-4/2-3 curve applies to sealed too. Mediocre 2-drop > 4th 5-drop payoff.
  • Cut ruthlessly: Last cuts hardest — cut high vanillas over tricks that save bombs.

Mana & fixing

Common duals + Hobbit Hole + Treasure = one single-pip splash reasonable. Double-pip (Bilbo’s Deadly Slice) is poor splash.

  • 2-color: 9/8 sources. + splash: 8/7/3-ish + treasures.
  • Don’t run 3 equal colors.
  • Treasure = fixing first, not ramp turn 2 (you get it later).

Sideboard plans

  • Bring flyers vs RW, artifact interaction vs Treasure/Equipment, exile vs recursion/Flashback.
  • On draw, cut a 2-drop for extra removal/trick.
  • Artifacts matter: Pinecone Strike & Burn, Burn get extra value popping Equipment/Axe.

6 · Prerelease Kit — 50-Minute Plan

⏱ Timed

In the box

  • 6 HOB Play Boosters
  • 1 foil rare/mythic promo
  • 4 full-art seasonal Hobbit basics (per collecting article — prerelease guide omits but box has them)
  • Deck box + Spindown

Clock

  1. 0–5: Open, sort by color, bombs/ removal face-up.
  2. 5–15: Count playables per pair.
  3. 15–28: Sleeve best 23 + 17 lands.
  4. 28–38: Table-test curve.
  5. 38–50: Sideboard stack + sleeves.

Checklist (taps to prog bar)

Sanity: can you cast a 2-drop on 2 in 85% of hands? If not, cut double-pip 2-drops or adjust lands.

Prerelease meta

Everyone learns — win by curving + holding instant tricks. New players tap out — hold Quarrel, Stone by Sunlight, Bofur trick, Settle the Wreckage (4 open white + all-in invite = punish). Bring dice, extra sleeves, water.

Removal hierarchy

  • Instant unconditional (Slice) > efficient damage+artifact hate (Pinecone) > modal protect/kill (Stone) > conditional/expensive (Thorin’s Last Stand, Magnificent End).
  • Need 4+ answers in sealed, 5+ in draft. Keep 1 artifact/enchantment answer in sideboard — maindeck dead G1, wins G2-3 vs Equipment.

Tricks to play around

  • Bofur // Concerted Care: hexproof+indestructible trick then lifelink body — don’t 2-for-1 yourself.
  • Stone by Sunlight: protect OR kill large — keep mana open.
  • Eagles Are Coming!: instant board transform.
  • Vow to Erebor in Dwarf Equipment — blowout.

8 · Traps — Don’t Die in Mirkwood

Wisdom

❌ Traps

  • Forcing typal: Elves/Dwarves need density payoffs; Humans/Wolves/Goblins care more about mechanic than just type. Don’t play bad tribe member to hit count.
  • Greedy splash: BB or RR splash with one Treasure = mulligans. Single-pip only.
  • Legendary artifact counts as 1: For storied, multi-qualifier = 1. Don’t double-count.
  • Overvaluing Sagas/Equip: Card advantage but slow — 2 Sagas max unless control. Ornate Equipment without cheap equip is tempo loss.
  • Recruit wrong: Don’t toss premium spell for 1/1 — use flashback/Adventure discard or when token matters.

✅ Pro tips

  • Play first if RW/BR aggro; draw if recruit/control.
  • Bait removal with medium threat before bomb (storied payoff, Eagle).
  • Hold Treasure sac instant — bluff bargain/recruit.
  • Count to 3 for storied before committing synergy card.
  • Sideboard hard — flyers vs tempo, exile vs recursion, artifact hate vs hoard.
