1 · Overview — Welcome to Middle-earth Limited

Read first • 4 min

Magic: The Gathering — The Hobbit is a Universes Beyond draftable set built for limited. Like Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth before it, expect a flavorful, synergy-driven environment where story moments (Bilbo’s riddles, Smaug, the Battle of Five Armies) are mechanics.

At a glance
  • Format: 40-card limited — 3×14-card Draft boosters or 6×14-card Sealed pool + prerelease kit
  • Games: Best-of-three with sideboarding — 16–18 lands typical
  • Speed: Preview season suggests midrange synergy > pure aggro (verify when full spoiler drops)
  • Fixing: Hobbit travel/treasure/food clues hint at light fixing — don’t force 3 colors early

Prerelease truth: You have 50 minutes to build. This guide is designed for that clock — don’t brew, execute. Sort by color, find your two deepest colors, check archetype bingo, then cut to 40.

What makes Hobbit limited different?

  • Story beats as mechanics — expect Party/Adventure/Food-like callbacks and new Hobbit-specific keywords (e.g., burgle/riddle/bargain variants). Value comes from doing the thing, not just stats.
  • Treasures & Dragons — LOTR showed Treasure as fixing + ramp; Smaug-style dragons likely top-end bombs that demand answers.
  • Halflings & Dwarves tribal soft synergies — typal payoffs at uncommon, not “only Dwarves” decks.

2 · Mechanics — What your cards are really doing

Deep dive
⌕

Hover a mechanic for limited translation — rules text → what it means for combat, deckbuilding, and pick priority.

🗝️ Burglar / Burgle — steal value when unblocked

What it is (preview): Hobbit-flavored saboteur mechanic — when a Burglar creature deals combat damage to a player, you burgle (exile/steal/treasure). Think Ninjutsu-lite without swapping.

In limited: Makes 1/3s matter. Draft evasive 1-drops higher. Hold tricks to push burgle through. Sideboard cheap blockers vs it.

Draft tip: Burglar wants evasion + protection. Prioritize 1-mana tricks that grant unblockable/hexproof over +2/+2.
❓ Riddle — mini-game / choices

What it is (preview): Inspired by Bilbo vs Gollum — opponent chooses, you reward. Expect “riddle — opponent guesses, if wrong you draw/tutor”.

In limited: Always generates value, but play around by choosing the less-punishing mode for opponent. Don’t overvalue riddle enablers early.

🐉 Hoard / Treasure — artifact ramp & fixing

Treasure tokens return as central fixing/splash enabler. Hoard likely counts treasures / dragon hoard size. Dragons care about hoard threshold.

LP: Treasure makes splashing a bomb free — but don’t count on it before turn 4. Still build 2-color base.

👥 Fellowship / Company — party of adventurers

Variant of Party — count unique creature types (Hobbit, Dwarf, Wizard, etc.). Payoffs scale 1→4. Commons will say “if you have a full Company”.

LP: Draft flexibly — don’t force all 4 types Pack 1. Value enablers (changelings/creatures with two types) rise a full pick.

🍖 Bargain / Food — sac for value

Food tokens and bargain (sac artifact/enchantment/treasure to buff) give grind. Green/black likely food producers.

LP: Food stalls aggro; bargain rewards you for cracking treasures late. Keep 1-2 sacrifice fodder lands/artifacts.

📜 Quest / Saga — chapters that pay over time

Expect Sagas for key story beats (An Unexpected Party, Riddles in the Dark, Fire and Water). Chapters = immediate + delayed value.

LP: Sagas are card advantage on one card — maindeck them, but curve out; don’t play 5 three-drops + saga.

4 · Draft — The 3-Pack Road

Booster Draft • 8 pods 40 cards • 16–18 lands

Pack 1 — Bread, but smarter

Classic BREAD still works, but adjust for Hobbit:

Pick orderWhatWhy here
S Bomb rare/mythicDragon, Planeswalker Gandalf-likeWins alone; treasure splashes it
A Premium removal3-mana kill, 2-mana bounce+riddleScarce; take over solid creature
B Efficient creature / signpost2-drop 2/2 burglar, Fellowship lordSignals archetype
C Filler / trick+2/+2 trick, 5-drop vanillaOnly if lane open
P1P1 heuristic: Take the best card that keeps you in 2 colors. Don’t first-pick a double-pip gold card unless it’s a bomb — you’ll lock yourself.

Curve math

  • Aim 6–7 one- and two-drops total. Hobbit burgle decks want 4+ one-drops.
  • 13–15 creatures minimum; 3–5 interaction; 2–4 tricks/combat tricks.
  • Keep your 2-color count 23 playables before considering splash.

Pack 2 — Find the open lane

  • By P1P8 you should know: what’s wheeling? What’s missing? If you see 0 good cards in your colors, pivot hard.
  • Take fixing (treasure makers, duals) over 6th playable if you’re solid.
  • Hate-draft only bombs that beat you — don’t hate filler.
✅ Stay open if:

You have 8+ playables across 2 colors and signals agree. Pass gold cards in other pairs happily.

🔄 Pivot if:

You cut a color Pack 1 and still see premium late. You have <5 playables in a color by pick 6.

Pack 3 — Fill holes & cut cleverly

  • Need 2-drops? Take commons over medium rares.
  • Count lands: 17 is default; 16 if curve <2.8 and treasures; 18 if you splash or curve >3.6.
  • Sideboard has value — duress, artifact hate, flying hate.

Build checklist

Signals — Reading the table

Signals = what you’re being passed. Late premium commons = that color is open.

5 · Sealed Deck — Build from Chaos

6 packs • 40 cards Slower • Bombier

Step 1 — Sort in 5 minutes

  1. Lay all 84 cards by color (WUBRG + multicolor + artifacts/lands). Face bombs up.
  2. Count playables per color — C- and above. Ignore unplayable filler.
  3. Count fixing: treasures, duals, ramps.
  4. Identify your two deepest colors + best splash.
Rule of 7: Need ~14 playables per color pair to make 23. If best pair has 17, you have a clean 2-color deck. If not, plan a splash.
I have a bomb in a shallow color — what then?

Splash it off treasures/dual if single-pip. Don’t warp mana for double-pip. Play 17 lands, 1 treasure maker = free splash.

Step 2 — Build the 40

  • Curve first: Sealed is slower — you can play more 4-5 drops than draft, but still need 6+ 2-drops.
  • Bombs > synergy: Unlike draft, your pool won’t have perfect Company payoffs — lean bombs + removal + best commons.
  • Cut ruthlessly: Last 2 cuts are hardest. Cut high-cost vanillas over tricks that save bombs.

23/17 skeleton

SlotCountNotes
Creatures13–16Include burglars even as 1/1s for pressure
Removal / tricks4–7Count sagas as creature+spell
Other (ramp/equipment)0–3Treasure makers go here
Lands17–1818 with splash or 6+ 5-drops

Mana & fixing

Use treasures as fixing, not ramp early. Duals prop you — play them even if tapped if you have 2-drops to smooth.

  • 2-color: 9/8 sources. 2-color + splash (single pip): 8/7/2 + treasures.
  • Don’t run 3 equal colors — pick 2 main + light third.

Sideboard plans

  • Bring anti-flyer cards in vs Fellowship tempo; bring exile/artifact hate vs Treasure hoard.
  • On draw, cut a 2-drop for an extra trick or removal.

6 · Prerelease Kit — Your 50-Minute Plan

⏱ Timed

What’s in the box (expected)

  • 6 Hobbit Draft Boosters (14 cards each)
  • 1 foil promo + spindown
  • Sturdy deckbox + insert guide

Clock

  1. 0–5 min: Open, sort by color, pull bombs/removal.
  2. 5–15 min: Count playables per color pair.
  3. 15–30 min: Sleeve best 23 + 17 lands.
  4. 30–40 min: Playtest curve on table.
  5. 40–50 min: Sideboard stack + grab snacks.

Interactive checklist

Sanity check: Can you cast a spell turn 2 in 85% of hands? If not, adjust lands or cut double-pip 2-drops.

Prerelease meta

Everyone is learning — punish by playing clean curves and instant-speed tricks. Hold burgle tricks for blowouts; new players tap out. Bring dice, extra sleeves, and water.

8 · Traps & Tips — Don’t Die in Mirkwood

Wisdom

❌ Common traps

  • Forcing Company: Need 7+ synergies; don’t play bad dwarves to hit count.
  • Greedy hoard: Splashing double-pip dragon with 1 treasure = mulligans.
  • Ignoring burglar: Letting 1/1 through for 3 turns loses to burgle snowball.
  • Playing all sagas: They’re strong but slow — 2 max unless you’re control.

✅ Pro tips

  • Play first if aggro/burglar; draw if grindy riddle/control.
  • Bait removal with medium threats before slamming bomb.
  • Hold treasure sac for instant speed — bluff bargain/riddle.
  • Sideboard aggressively — Hobbit likely has narrow hosers that are gold post-board.

One-page cheat sheet
