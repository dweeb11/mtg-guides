BO1 ≠ BO3. Hand smoother helps land count, not colors. No sideboard = BO1 rewards flexible answers (Erode, Bitter Triumph, Avatar's Wrath, Prismari Charm) and punishes narrow hate (Duress, sweeper, GY hate draw the wrong half). Sweepers/graveyard decks get free game-one equity; linear decks can't transform.

BO1 vs BO3 — deck construction
  • Smoother: BO1 biases opening hand land/spell ratio (multiple candidates) — helps land count, not colors/curve/synergy. MTGA Zone
  • Aggro skew: Proactive plans win before opponent knows what to hold → Mono-G/Auras/Prowess/Lifegain overperform vs tournament.
  • No sideboard correction: GY/artifact/enchantment/combo get free game-one equity; narrow maindeck (Duress/sweeper/High Noon/GY hate) draws wrong half.
  • Construction: Favor flexible interaction (Erode, Bitter Triumph, Avatar's Wrath, Prismari Charm) + independently functional cards. Don't trim lands assuming smoother guarantees land 3-4.
  • BO3 upside: Excruciator/Tempo/4c Control transform after game one (sweepers, counters, GY hate); linear decks face hated games 2-3.
