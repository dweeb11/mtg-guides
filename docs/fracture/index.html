1 · Overview — What FRA actually is

Start here • 3 min

Magic: The Gathering — Reality Fracture (FRA) — large, 10-archetype, all-formats-legal Echoverse set. 270 main-rarity cards (71 common / 109 uncommon / 64 rare / 26 mythic) + 10 common duals + 10 basics = 280 Draft cards (collectors 1–280). Scryfall's 461 counts printings/treatments, not unique draftables. Companion codes: FRC (Commander) + SPG (Special Guests, eternal-only except reprints). Preview debut Sep 8, full gallery Sep 18, prerelease Sep 25–Oct 1, Arena Sep 29, tabletop Oct 2.

At a glance
  • Boosters: 14-card Play Boosters (Draft = 3, Sealed = 6)
  • Format: 40 cards, 23 spells / 17 lands, BO3 with sideboard
  • Draft mode: Designed for Pick-Two (4-player Draft Night kit); Arena also Pick-One Premier/Traditional
  • Speed: Average curve, long grindy games — cheap interaction suppresses snowballing, then Jace + prepared spells fund 6–8 mana plays
  • Fixing: 10 planeswalker-conditional duals + ⧉ Room of Refuge + 5 basic landcyclers + Heartwood ramp — splash one single-pip, not double-pip
How to use this guide
  1. Read Mechanics — real reminder text so you know what cards do.
  2. Pick path top-right — highlights your lane.
  3. At table, open Prerelease Kit checklist on phone.
  4. During draft, keep Cards & Removal open for pick-order ties.

Press / to search. Click any archetype to expand. Progress auto-saves.

What makes FRA different: Unusually uncommon-heavy (commons cut 81→71, uncommons up 100→109) plus echoed-pair collation — every Play Booster has a linked pair of legends (pushed into different decks) plus a third legend. Uncommons and legends punch above their usual weight. SPG cards opened in Play Boosters are legal in that Draft/Sealed pool. Note regional product delays (NZ prereleases rescheduled; Mexico stock early Oct) — availability varies.

2 · Mechanics — Real rules, limited translation

Verified vs. release notes
⌕

Headline mechanics from Reality Fracture Mechanics + Release Notes + update bulletin. “Limited” = pick / play advice.

◈ Empower Jace N — marquee mechanic

Reminder: “Put N loyalty counters on a Jace token you control. If you don't control one, first create a blue Jace planeswalker token with '−1: Surveil 1' and '−3: Draw a card.'”

  • Only a Jace planeswalker token qualifies — a nontoken Jace doesn't count. Created at 0 loyalty, then counters added. Multiple Jaces? Choose one.
  • Empower never makes a second Jace while you control one. Token is blue, nonlegendary.

Limited: The set's shared engine — any deck can run it. Default line is −3 to draw; save loyalty only for a Way payoff, repeated empower, or tactical need. −1 surveil finds lands/answers or banks value before Jace dies. Pressure low-loyalty enemy Jaces early, then prioritize life total + board once attacks get inefficient.

⬢ Heartwood tokens — artifact mana

Definition: Red and green artifact token with “{T}: Add {R} or {G}.” (Some physical printings use a colorless frame — rules/reminder text make them red-green.)

Limited: Persistent ramp + artifact count. Core of RG Konstrari (⧉ Soul Tether makes them) but splashable fixing anywhere green/red-adjacent. Enables the format's big 6–8 mana plays.

⧉ Prepare — returning mechanic

Operation: A permanent that becomes prepared creates a copy of its associated spell in exile; controller may cast the copy (pay costs, choose targets). Casting makes it unprepared. Copy vanishes if the permanent leaves or becomes unprepared. Already-prepared can't re-prepare. New controller of a prepared permanent may cast the copy.

Limited: Delayed card advantage, not free spells — mana must still be paid, and killing the preparer deletes the exiled copy. Each allied school repeats one prepare spell across its common + uncommon preparers (see Archetypes). Treat as synergy/value pieces, not standalone bombs. Remove prepared creatures before the spell is cast when tempo allows.

✦ Way of the… — uncommon legendary enchantments

Ten uncommons (one per pair) that grant abilities to planeswalkers — materially raising Jace loyalty value. Significantly overperformed in early access; prioritize them once in a lane.

Limited: A Way + any empower source turns every Jace into a repeat engine. Sideboard enchantment removal more readily than usual (Ways + ⧉ Memory Trap).

◐ Echoed pairs — collation structure, not a keyword

Every Play Booster contains a linked pair of legends + a third legend; paired cards intentionally support different decks so adjacent drafters aren't pushed into the same lane. Sealed pools can concentrate uncommon/rare power.

Limited: Don't read a pack's legend pair as “my lane is X” — they're designed to split. In sealed, expect spiky pools.

▤ Supporting themes

Surveil, threshold (7 cards), prowess, flashback, landfall, basic landcycling, lifegain, +1/+1 counters, graveyard recursion, artifact payoffs — each anchors 1–2 archetypes rather than floating free.

Limited: Five large commons have basic landcycling (⧉ Hexhaven Battalion, ⧉ Undulating Witness, ⧉ Apex Witchstalker, ⧉ Awaken the Inferno, ⧉ Vinelasher Adept) — far better in Sealed (fix early, threaten late) than Draft.

Sources: Wizards Mechanics, Release Notes, update bulletin, prerelease guide, design articles (Echoverse + Reality Check pt.2).

3 · Archetypes — Ten Real Decks

Wizards-confirmed • 5+5Pick-Two draft

Wizards confirms ten supported pairs: five allied = Hexhaven schools, five enemy = the Lorwyn Five. Each allied school repeats one prepare spell; each pair has a common hybrid + a planeswalker-conditional dual (tapped unless you control a planeswalker — a Jace token turns later copies on).

Filter:
◈ hexhaven schools allied pairs · military-academy register
W U — WU Fatehold: Surveil Aggro

Signpost: ⧉ Desperate Futurescribe · Hybrid: ⧉ Fatehold Chronologist · Prepare: ⧉ Peer Review (2/2 Cadet + surveil 1) via Chronologist / ⧉ Prudent Fateseer · Land: ⧉ Fatehold Annex

Plan: Scry/surveil, then grow evasive attackers. Chronologist doubles as flexible early pickup for either color.

U B — UB Theorix: Graveyard Math

Signpost: ⧉ Recursive Recruitment · Hybrid: ⧉ Theorix Metamage · Prepare: ⧉ Omit Variables (mill 3) via Metamage / ⧉ Paradox Shaper · Land: ⧉ Theorix Annex

Plan: Self-mill to 7-card threshold, then out-value. One of the clearest self-contained engines — safe early lane.

B R — BR Stingerquill: Face Burn

Signpost: ⧉ Grim Repriser · Hybrid: ⧉ Whiplash Wordsmith · Prepare: ⧉ Vicious Verse (1 to opponent) via Wordsmith / ⧉ Stingerquill Voxmancer · Land: ⧉ Stingerquill Annex

Plan: Noncombat damage + prowess + recursion. Clear engine, but don't take Voxmancer as a reason to enter BR — it's support, not a bomb.

R G — RG Konstrari: Mana Ramp

Signpost: ⧉ Craftwork Crusher · Hybrid: ⧉ Konstrari Improviser · Prepare: ⧉ Soul Tether (make a Heartwood) via Improviser / ⧉ Woodwork Prodigy · Land: ⧉ Konstrari Annex

Plan: Heartwood/artifact ramp into large threats. Self-contained engine; green improved sharply after prerelease play.

G W — GW Vigorbloom: Life Gain

Signpost: ⧉ Bloombrute · Hybrid: ⧉ Emergency Phytomedic · Prepare: ⧉ Seed Suture (+1/+1 counter, gain 1) via Phytomedic / ⧉ Vigorbloom Vanguard · Land: ⧉ Vigorbloom Annex

Plan: Lifegain plus +1/+1 counters — grindy board growth that goes over the top.

◐ the lorwyn five enemy pairs · character-led counterpart spreads
W B — WB Liliana's Attrition

Signpost: ⧉ Twisted Fates · Hybrid: ⧉ Blessed Ghoul · Land: ⧉ Meticulous Commons

Plan: Small creatures, sacrifice, recursion — bleed the opponent out. Enemy pair: needs its uncommons, not just on-theme commons.

U R — UR Chandra's Prowess

Signpost: ⧉ Clash of Elements · Hybrid: ⧉ Twinned Vision (breakout common) · Land: ⧉ Innovative Commons

Plan: Noncreature spells + tempo/burn. ⧉ Twinned Vision is a high-priority flexible pickup.

B G — BG Garruk's Bestiary

Signpost: ⧉ Primal Witchstalker · Hybrid: ⧉ Ferocity of the Hunt (flash Aura) · Land: ⧉ Formidable Commons

Plan: Value creatures with deathtouch/trample + recursion. Straightforward card-quality deck when synergies miss.

R W — RW Ajani's Army

Signpost: ⧉ Warrior's Blades · Hybrid: ⧉ Charge the Sanctum · Land: ⧉ Dedicated Commons

Plan: Cadet tokens + counter aggro — go wide, pump, punish stumbles. Red's efficient removal is the format's most consistently praised strength.

G U — GU Jace's Mastery

Signpost: ⧉ Mind Meanderer · Hybrid: ⧉ Tam's Resistance · Land: ⧉ Transformative Commons

Plan: Empower Jace + granted loyalty abilities (Ways). Highest ceiling, highest demand — requires uncommon hooks, not just on-theme commons. Don't force without payoffs.

Fixing: All ten duals enter tapped unless you control a planeswalker — make a Jace token and later copies come in untapped. ⧉ Room of Refuge is a bonus common 5-color fixer + late counter source. Supports single-pip splashes, not 5-color. Draft is harder to splash than Sealed (many premiums are double-pipped).
Pivot advice — original / echoverse: Start with powerful rares, uncommons, flexible removal, and good bodies — not an early common forcing a school. Common hybrids + planeswalker-duals let you settle into the actually-open lane. BR, UB, and RG have the clearest self-contained engines if you need a default.

P1P1: Best card that keeps you in one color — avoid double-pip gold unless bomb. Early consensus: red first, blue second, black last pre-prerelease; post-early-access says every color viable, green up sharply (provisional — Arena launched today, no 17Lands yet).

Curve math (Wizards + data)

  • Wizards curve: 1–2 one-drops, 7–8 two-drops, 5–6 three-drops, 3–4 four-drops, 2–3 five-drops, 0–1 six-plus, 17 lands
  • ~68% of spells cost ≤3, yet games reach 6–8 mana via Jace + prepared spells — don't cut all top end. 3 damage kills ~68% of creatures; 32% have toughness 4+.
  • Pick-Two: you see more cards, commit slightly faster than normal draft.

Pack 2 — Find open lane

  • By P1P8: what's wheeling? Zero good cards in your colors → pivot to neighbor sharing a color (hybrids make this cheap).
  • Take fixing (duals, Heartwood makers, ⧉ Room of Refuge) once you have 8+ playables in pair.
  • Enemy pairs (esp. GU) need uncommon hooks — don't commit on commons alone.
  • Hate only bombs that beat you — not filler.
Stay if

8+ playables across pair, signals agree, you're passing other pairs' gold freely.

Pivot if

<5 playables by pick 6 in a color, or late premium removal still wheeling elsewhere.

Pack 3 — Fill holes & cut

  • Need 2-drops? Take commons over medium rares. Landcyclers are mediocre here — leave them.
  • 17 lands default; 16 if curve <2.8 + Heartwood; 18 if splash or heavy top end.
  • Sideboard: enchantment removal (Ways, ⧉ Memory Trap), planeswalker answers, flying hate — BO3 matters.

Signals in Pick-Two

Late premium = open lane. Pick-Two doubles info per pack — late cards are stronger signal. Remember echoed pairs split legends across decks: a pack's two legends pointing different ways is normal, not a signal.

5 · Sealed — Build from Chaos

6×14 • 40 cards

Sort in 5 minutes

  1. Lay pool by color (WUBRG + multi + artifact/land). Bombs + evasive threats face-up.
  2. Count playables ≥C per color. Ignore filler — small vanilla ground creatures underperform here.
  3. Count fixing: duals, ⧉ Room of Refuge, Heartwood makers, landcyclers.
  4. Find two deepest colors + best single-pip splash. Watch for echoed-pair concentration (spiky uncommon/rare power).
Rule: ~14 playables per pair to make 23. Best pair 17+ = clean 2-color. Else plan a careful splash. Don't force the printed archetype if raw cards point elsewhere.

Build the 40

  • Bombs > synergy: Pools won't deliver perfect engines — lean bombs + removal + best commons.
  • Slower than the curve diagram implies: strong 6–7 drops are playable, especially with landcycling or Heartwood. Mediocre 2-drop still beats 4th 5-drop payoff, though.
  • Count prepared spells as delayed cards, not free spells — the mana must still be paid.
  • Cut ruthlessly: last cuts hardest — cut high vanillas over tricks that save bombs.

Mana & fixing

Duals + ⧉ Room of Refuge + landcyclers + Heartwood = one careful splash, or even 2 colors + splash over greedy 3-color. Expensive landcyclers are much better in Sealed than Draft (fix early, threaten late).

  • 2-color: 9/8 sources. + splash: 8/7/3-ish + Heartwood.
  • Don't run 3 equal colors. Double-pip splashes are mulligans waiting to happen.

Sideboard plans

  • Bring enchantment removal more readily than usual — ⧉ Memory Trap + powerful Ways.
  • Answers that hit planeswalkers pull double duty vs Jace tokens.
  • On draw, cut a 2-drop for extra removal/trick.

6 · Prerelease Kit — 50-Minute Plan

⏱ Timed

In the box

  • 6 FRA Play Boosters
  • 1 traditional-foil promo (play it if on-color)
  • Deck box + Spindown die
  • 90 basics come from the store, not the box — ask early

Clock

  1. 0–5: Open, sort by color, bombs/removal face-up.
  2. 5–15: Count playables per pair; note echoed-pair spikes.
  3. 15–28: Sleeve best 23 + 17 lands.
  4. 28–38: Table-test curve; check 2-drop-on-2.
  5. 38–50: Sideboard stack + sleeves.

Checklist (taps to prog bar)

Sanity: can you cast a 2-drop on 2 in 85% of hands? If not, cut double-pip 2-drops or adjust lands.

Prerelease meta

Everyone learns — win by curving + holding instant tricks. New players tap out: hold ⧉ Last Gasp, ⧉ Wrath of the Bloodmane, ⧉ Unsummon effects, and ⧉ Charge the Sanctum. Bring dice, extra sleeves, water.

Removal hierarchy

  • Instant unconditional (⧉ Last Gasp) > efficient damage (⧉ Wrath of the Bloodmane) > modal/conditional (⧉ Memory Trap, ⧉ Compel Brutality) > expensive/situational.
  • Need 4+ answers in sealed, 5+ in draft. Keep 1 enchantment answer in sideboard — dead G1, wins G2–3 vs Ways/Memory Trap.

Tricks to play around (open-mana warnings)

  • White: ⧉ Prophesied End (cleanest vs attacker — else they draw) · ⧉ Your Fate Ends Here · ⧉ Academic Ascent
  • Blue: ⧉ Unsummon · ⧉ Icy Reception · ⧉ Perfected Theory · ⧉ Countersculpt — don't expose two cards to bounce after an Aura/trick
  • Black: ⧉ Last Gasp (turns combat even when −3/−3 doesn't kill) · ⧉ Essence Burn
  • Red: ⧉ Wrath of the Bloodmane · ⧉ Blazing Crescendo
  • Green: ⧉ Tethermage's Advantage (untaps — tapped creature can still block!) · ⧉ Compel Brutality · ⧉ Flourishing Grapple
  • Multi/hybrid: ⧉ Fatehold Charm (bounces creature or spell) · ⧉ Konstrari Charm · ⧉ Charge the Sanctum · flash Aura ⧉ Ferocity of the Hunt (deathtouch; returns creature tapped on death)

8 · Traps — Don't Crack the Multiverse

Wisdom

✕ traps

  • Forcing a school P1: ten lanes + echoed-pair splitting means early commons lie. Start with rares/uncommons/removal/bodies, settle via hybrids + duals.
  • Greedy double-pip splash in draft: single-pip only — BB/RR splashes with one Heartwood = mulligans.
  • Treating prepared spells as free: the mana must still be paid, and killing the preparer deletes the copy. Cast-or-lose timing matters.
  • Auto-attacking every Jace forever: pressure low-loyalty Jaces early, then develop — throwing attacks into a stabilized board loses races.
  • ⧉ Command the Stage goggles: build-around and Voxmancer-as-reason-to-enter both graded down in early access. Take the engine, not the dream.
  • No enchantment removal post-board: Ways overperformed + ⧉ Memory Trap is premium — board answers G2.

◆ pro tips

  • Default Jace line: −3 draw now. Bank loyalty only for a Way, repeated empower, or a tactical turn.
  • −1 surveil to find land #4, dig for an answer, enable surveil payoffs, or extract value before Jace dies.
  • Remove prepared creatures before the exiled spell is cast when tempo permits.
  • Landcyclers: pass in draft, snap in sealed (fix early, threaten late).
  • Sideboard hard — enchantment removal, walker answers, flying hate.
